## Interview plan

Target: 10–15 owners or managers in Pune, with a mix of property sizes. This is enough to find repeated problems, not to prove market size.

Rules:
- Ask about **past behaviour**, not opinions about a future product. "When did this last happen? What did you do?" beats "Would you use an app that…?"
- Do not pitch StaySphere until the end, if at all.
- A compliment, a demo request, or "I'd try it" is not evidence of willingness to pay.

Questions:
1. How many properties, rooms, and beds do you run? Who helps you?
2. Walk me through a normal month. Where does your time go?
3. When a resident moves in, what happens, step by step? What goes wrong?
4. How do you know which beds are free right now? When was that last wrong?
5. How do you collect rent? How much was late or unpaid last month? How do you chase it?
6. How do you plan how much food to cook? What happens to leftovers?
7. When something breaks, how does the resident tell you? How long until it's fixed?
8. What tools do you use today (WhatsApp, Excel, notebook, any app)? Have you paid for any software? Why did you keep or stop it?
9. Which of these problems, if solved, would save you the most money or time?
10. If something solved that, what would you expect to pay per month?

Record per interview: date, property size, top 3 problems in the owner's words, current workaround, money or time lost (their estimate), tools used, willingness-to-pay signal.

## Decision after interviews

Rank problems by **frequency × cost × how much they already spend working around them**. Phase order after Phase 3 (rent records vs. maintenance vs. meals) follows that ranking, not this document's assumptions.

| Active owner (weekly) | Performed at least one state-changing action that week | Logged in |
| Resident activation | Accepted the invitation and signed in at least once | Invited |
